## 🚨 RED FLAGS - ESCALATE IMMEDIATELY

**If you notice ANY of these, take action:**

```
RED FLAG #1: ANYONE SLEEPING <3 HOURS LAST NIGHT
├─ Action: Talk to that person
├─ Offer: Light duties today, early rest tonight
├─ Monitor: Check again tomorrow
└─ Escalate if: Continues <3 hours for multiple nights

RED FLAG #2: STRESS LEVEL CRITICAL (visibly stressed/panicked)
├─ Action: Talk to person in private
├─ Offer: "What do you need to manage this?" 
├─ Options: Reassign some tasks, extra break, talk to counselor
└─ Escalate if: Not improving after support

RED FLAG #3: DECISION-MAKING DEGRADING (Project Manager/CTO level)
├─ Action: Observe decision quality
├─ Offer: "Do you want me to take over this decision?"
├─ Monitor: Is fatigue affecting judgment?
└─ Escalate if: Critical decisions being made poorly

RED FLAG #4: ALERT FATIGUE (Monitoring Lead getting desensitized)
├─ Signs: Ignoring alerts, delayed response, dismissive attitude
├─ Action: Rotate monitoring duties if possible
├─ Offer: "You've been alert-watching for 6 hours, let's rotate"
└─ Escalate if: Missing actual issues

RED FLAG #5: MORALE PLUMMETING
├─ Signs: Negative talk, team frustration, questioning plan
├─ Action: Acknowledge difficulty, celebrate wins, remind why we're here
├─ Offer: "This is hard, and you're doing great"
└─ Escalate if: Morale not recovering

RED FLAG #6: TEAM MEMBER EXPRESSING BURNOUT
├─ Phrases to listen for: "I can't", "I'm too tired", "I'm done", "This is impossible"
├─ Action: Listen, validate, offer support
├─ Offer: Rest, reassignment, professional support
└─ Escalate if: Person not able to continue

RED FLAG #7: MULTIPLE TEAM MEMBERS AT RISK
├─ If 2+ people showing critical signs
├─ Action: Call CTO/Project Manager for assessment
├─ Offer: Bring in backup team members
└─ Escalate: May need to slow deployment if team at risk
```

## 🎯 TEAM CAPACITY PLANNING

**Adjust work based on team capacity:**

```
IF TEAM HEALTH SCORE ≥20:
  └─ Proceed with normal Phase execution
     Team has capacity for normal workload
     Continue current pace

IF TEAM HEALTH SCORE 15-19:
  └─ Maintain current Phase, but monitor closely
     Team is managing but showing signs of strain
     Don't increase workload
     Increase support/recognition

IF TEAM HEALTH SCORE 10-14:
  └─ SLOW deployment pace
     "We're going to reduce scope for today"
     Focus on team health over speed
     Get everyone more rest
     Bring in extra help if available

IF TEAM HEALTH SCORE <10:
  └─ EMERGENCY team recovery
     Call CTO/Project Manager
     "Team needs break, we need to pause/adjust"
     Consider: Light duties, rest day, backup support
     May need to delay deployment progress
```

## 🏆 TEAM SUSTAINABILITY GOAL

**The goal is NOT to burn out the team.**  
**The goal is to sustainably deliver May 1-21 deployment successfully.**

Teams that are:
- Well-rested (5+ hours)
- Well-fed (regular meals, hydration)
- Recognized (daily acknowledgment)
- Supported (help with issues)
- Moving (hourly breaks)

→ These teams execute better
→ These teams make fewer mistakes
→ These teams actually faster
→ These teams don't burn out

**Your job as Operations Lead:**

Facilitate operational performance AND team health.
Not one or the other. Both.

A healthy team is a performant team.
